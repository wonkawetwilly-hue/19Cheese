Affinage is the work

Anyone can make curd. What happens for the next nine months decides whether it becomes cheese or compost. Ours happens on slate shelving in a cellar cut into the hillside behind the shop, at 92% humidity, turned by hand, with the rind brushed every four days.

We buy young wheels from farms within forty kilometres and take it from there. It is slow, it is boring, and it is the entire point.

  • 01 Raw milk only — no heat treatment above 40 °C.
  • 02 Rind brushed and turned every four days.
  • 03 Salt measured by weight, never by feel.
  • 04 Sold at peak, or not at all.
